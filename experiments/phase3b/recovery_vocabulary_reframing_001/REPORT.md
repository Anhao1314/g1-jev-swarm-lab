# Phase 3B.0b — Recovery Vocabulary Reframing

**Verdict: `RECOVERY_VOCABULARY_NOT_READY`.** Existing experiments identify useful control mechanisms, but none yet supplies a distinct, Jev-selectable recovery with a positive and a negative/no-need state under one matched decision contract. The Phase 3B.0a `CONTINUE / ABSTAIN / ABSTAIN` result remains frozen. This is a read-only architecture study, not a new controller result or a new Jev benchmark.

## Decision boundary and evidence handling

The question is whether a bounded **control strategy**, rather than a raw residual vector, has demonstrated state-dependent selection value. The [source manifest](source_manifest.json) pins 19 retained reports, decisions and contracts plus three raw predecision snapshots reviewed at source commit `f42a5bf`. Phase 1 and Phase 3 studies differ in controller, reference, intervention timing and scoring. Their individual observations can motivate a mode abstraction, but cannot be inserted into one Oracle outcome cell or combined to claim a strict + physical + same-reference global witness. Physical PASS below means tested MuJoCo physical success, not hardware safety.

## Decision-time observability

The [frozen Phase 3B state contract](../../phase3b/decision_oracle_001/contract.json) includes transition/skill, local and route position/heading error, reference heading, instantaneous strict margin, remaining distance and previous recovery. In Phase 3B.0a, route Y and heading distinguish all three states before action: Stand5 versus historical changes route Y by +0.12175m and route heading by +2.74048°; push60 versus historical changes route Y by +0.19576m and route heading by −0.65889°. Actual-start local lateral error is 0 and strict margin 0.28m for all three, so those two instantaneous fields hide the differences.

The retained decision snapshots also expose base twist. The three `qvel` Y components at first-Walk entry are approximately −0.123, −0.043 and +0.560m/s (historical, Stand5, push60); yaw-rate components are +0.079, −0.007 and −0.132rad/s. A **minimal candidate increment** for a future state schema is signed route-frame planar velocity plus yaw rate from this predecision snapshot. It would represent momentum after a push, but its ability to improve mode selection is unproven because the current matrix has no recovery-positive label. Short error trend may become useful if a future decision is made after motion starts; no retained matched selection result establishes its necessity now. Remaining route is constant at 8m and previous recovery is null in these three states, so they offer no current discrimination, though route length and recovery history remain sensible bounded context fields. No frozen schema is changed by this study.

## Readiness and stop

The current matched benchmark has a `CONTINUE` state and two `ABSTAIN` states, but **zero unique admissible recovery states**. Phase 1.3 provides a credible feedback mechanism, not an independent recovery choice on top of the Phase 3 controller. Reference alignment and reorientation do not have a matched, strict+physical+same-reference-global positive cell. Thus there is no evidence-backed bounded Jev recovery vocabulary to freeze or rebuild into a Jev benchmark. The [decision record](decision.json) keeps candidate status separate from an authorized mode contract.

This study used existing evidence only: zero physics executions, Jev calls, PPO runs, residual/reward changes or Runtime integration. Stop here. A future protocol would first have to choose one strategy abstraction and obtain matched same-state outcomes with explicit off, recovery and no-admissible controls; this report does not authorize that acquisition.
